Gantt Scheduling Logic

Greedy LPT Active
The "Why": The system implements the Longest Processing Time (LPT) algorithm. By sorting tasks from largest to smallest, we front-load the most demanding workloads. This prevents "bottleneck tasks" from being left for the end, which is the primary cause of core idling in standard FIFO schedulers.
Heat Dissipation Index
H = w \cdot e^{\alpha \cdot f}

Thermal Safety Status

-- °C (Safe)
The "Why": Thermal stress is calculated using the Alpha (α) coefficient. Since heat increases exponentially with frequency (f), the system monitors real-time dissipation. If the estimated heat ratio exceeds safe thresholds, the status flips to 'WARNING' to prevent permanent silicon degradation.
Efficiency Delta
\Delta = \frac{T_{FIFO} - T_{LPT}}{T_{FIFO}}

Efficiency Gain

-- % Gain
The "Why": This value represents the Workload Balance Efficiency. It compares our LPT results against a naive FIFO scheduler. The gain exists because we've minimized the "empty space" (idle time) on some cores while others are still running, ensuring all M Cores finish as close to each other as possible.
Power & EDP Laws
P = M \cdot e^{\alpha \cdot f}, \text{EDP} = E \cdot T

Power Law Compliance

-- Watts
The "Why": Processor power consumption follows an Exponential Penalty curve. By choosing an optimal frequency (f), we minimize the Energy-Delay Product (EDP), avoiding the vertical climb in power usage where battery drain triples for only a marginal gain in clock speed.
